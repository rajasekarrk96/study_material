# Engineering Mathematics & Applied Statistics — Master Syllabus

**Target Role:** AI Engineer / Data Scientist / Robotics Developer / Quantitative Analyst  
**Difficulty Level:** Intermediate  
**Estimated Duration:** 60 Hours  
**Prerequisites:** High School Mathematics  
**Required Courses:** None  
**Optional Courses:** python-data-science  

---

## Study Flow

### 1. Engineering Mathematics

#### 1.1. Module 1 — Linear Algebra for Computing

1. **Vectors, Matrices & Linear Transformations**
    - **Course Coverage:** 🟢 Covered in Class
    - Vector Spaces, Subspaces, and Basis Systems
    - Matrix Operations, Transpose, and Determinants
    - Systems of Linear Equations & Gaussian Elimination
    - Linear Transformations & Eigenvalues / Eigenvectors
2. **Matrix Decompositions & Dimensionality Reduction**
    - **Course Coverage:** 🟢 Covered in Class
    - Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)
    - Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Math
    - LU Decomposition and Cholesky Factorization
    - Vector Norms (L1, L2, Frobenius Norm)

#### 1.2. Module 2 — Multivariable Calculus & Optimization

1. **Differential & Integral Calculus**
    - **Course Coverage:** 🟢 Covered in Class
    - Partial Derivatives and Gradient Vectors
    - Directional Derivatives and Hessian Matrices
    - Chain Rule for Vector Functions (Backpropagation Calculus)
    - Multivariable Integration and Change of Variables
2. **Optimization Algorithms**
    - **Course Coverage:** 🟢 Covered in Class
    - Convex Optimization & Convex Sets
    - Gradient Descent, Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD), Adam
    - Constrained Optimization & Lagrange Multipliers
    - Global vs Local Minima in High-Dimensional Loss Surfaces

#### 1.3. Module 3 — Applied Probability & Statistics

1. **Probability Foundations & Distributions**
    - **Course Coverage:** 🟢 Covered in Class
    - Discrete vs Continuous Random Variables
    - Probability Mass Functions (PMF) and Probability Density Functions (PDF)
    - Gaussian (Normal), Binomial, Poisson, and Exponential Distributions
    - Expectation, Variance, and Covariance Matrices
2. **Statistical Inference & Hypothesis Testing**
    - **Course Coverage:** 🟢 Covered in Class
    - Central Limit Theorem (CLT) & Sampling Distributions
    - Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) & Bayesian Inference
    - Hypothesis Testing: p-values, t-tests, Chi-Square Tests, ANOVA
    - Confidence Intervals and Margin of Error Calculation
