# Soldering Techniques

> **Course**: Electronics Basics | **Module**: Practical Electronics | **Difficulty**: beginner

---

Soldering creates reliable metallurgical and electrical joints between electronic components and PCB pads using molten filler metal.

---



---

Soldering relies on wetting: molten solder alloy (60/40 Lead-Tin or SAC300 Lead-Free) dissolves a microscopic layer of copper to form an intermetallic bond. Flux removes copper oxides during heating to allow clean solder flow.

---

Soldering Temperatures:
Leaded Solder (Sn63/Pb37)   : Melt Temp = 183°C, Iron Set = 315°C - 340°C
Lead-Free (SAC305)          : Melt Temp = 217°C, Iron Set = 350°C - 370°C

Ideal Joint Anatomy:
- Concave fillet shape (like a shiny volcano)
- Smooth 45° wetting angle against component lead and copper pad

---

### 4-Step Through-Hole Soldering Technique

1. Clean PCB pad and component lead with isopropyl alcohol.
2. Apply heated iron tip simultaneously to BOTH the copper pad and component lead for 1.5 - 2 seconds.
3. Feed solder wire into the JUNCTION of pad and tip (NOT directly onto the iron tip) until concave fillet forms.
4. Remove solder wire first, then remove iron tip; allow joint to cool undisturbed for 3 seconds.

---

1. **Cold Solder Joint**: Moving component while cooling or insufficient heat creates dull, grainy, high-resistance unreliable joint.
2. **Solder Bridges**: Excess solder shorts adjacent fine-pitch pins; fix using desoldering braid/wick and flux.
3. **Thermal Damage to ICs**: Applying iron tip longer than 5 seconds overheats semiconductor die.

---

**Q1: Why is flux essential in soldering?**
A: Flux removes surface oxidation from metals when heated, preventing re-oxidation so solder wets cleanly.

**Q2: What causes a 'Cold Joint'?**
A: Insufficient heat transfer or physical movement during solder solidification.

**Q3: How do you clean flux residue from a finished PCB?**
A: Scrub with 99% Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) and an ESD-safe brush.

**Q4: What is Desoldering Braid (Wick)?**
A: Finely woven copper wire pre-coated with flux that absorbs molten unwanted solder via capillary action.
