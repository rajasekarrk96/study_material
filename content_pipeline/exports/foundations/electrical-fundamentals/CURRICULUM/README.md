# Curriculum: Electrical Fundamentals

## Course Overview
- **Course Name:** Electrical Fundamentals
- **Course Description:** A foundation course on Electrical Engineering, covering fundamentals of electricity, SI units, basic electrical quantities, DC circuits, components, AC fundamentals, safety, machines, power supplies, wiring, and practical troubleshooting.
- **Category:** Foundation Course
- **Learning Paths:**
  - IoT Full Stack Engineering
  - Embedded Systems Engineering
  - Electronics & PCB Design
  - Robotics Engineering
  - Electrical & Electronics Engineering
- **Estimated Hours:** 80 Hours
- **Total Modules:** 10
- **Total Lessons:** 34
- **Total Topics:** 230+
- **Curriculum Status:** READY_FOR_AUTHORING
