# Curriculum: Electronics Basics

## Course Overview
- **Course Name:** Electronics Basics
- **Course Description:** A foundation course on Electronics Basics, covering fundamentals of electronics, semiconductors, passive components, diodes, transistors, power electronics, analog/digital electronics, sensors, and PCB prototyping.
- **Category:** Foundation Course
- **Learning Paths:**
  - IoT Full Stack Engineering
  - Embedded Systems Engineering
  - Electronics & PCB Design
  - Robotics Engineering
  - Electrical & Electronics Engineering
- **Estimated Hours:** 80 Hours
- **Total Modules:** 10
- **Total Lessons:** 34
- **Total Topics:** 240+
- **Curriculum Status:** READY_FOR_AUTHORING
