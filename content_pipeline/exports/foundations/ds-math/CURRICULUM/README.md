# Curriculum: Data Science Mathematics

## Course Overview
- **Course Name:** Data Science Mathematics
- **Course Description:** A foundation course on Data Science Mathematics, covering mathematical foundations, linear algebra, calculus, probability, statistics, optimization mathematics, information theory, and mathematical foundations of ML/DL.
- **Category:** Foundation Course
- **Learning Paths:**
  - Data Science & Analytics
  - AI Engineering
  - Machine Learning
  - Deep Learning
  - Computer Vision
  - NLP & Generative AI
- **Estimated Hours:** 80 Hours
- **Total Modules:** 10
- **Total Lessons:** 31
- **Total Topics:** 220+
- **Curriculum Status:** READY_FOR_AUTHORING
