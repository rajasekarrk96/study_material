# Curriculum: Sensors & Actuators

## Course Overview
- **Course Name:** Sensors & Actuators
- **Course Description:** An intermediate technology course on Sensors & Actuators, covering sensor characteristics, environmental, motion, optical, biomedical sensors, actuator types, relay systems, motor control, industrial sensors/actuators, and integration projects.
- **Category:** Technology Course
- **Learning Paths:**
  - IoT Full Stack Engineering
  - Embedded Systems Engineering
  - Robotics Engineering
  - Industrial Automation
- **Estimated Hours:** 80 Hours
- **Total Modules:** 10
- **Total Lessons:** 33
- **Total Topics:** 210+
- **Curriculum Status:** READY_FOR_AUTHORING
